How it works

01 — Data

150 synthetic transactions

A noisy, probability-based ground-truth label — risk signals correlate with fraud but don't guarantee it, so the test is meaningful. 70/30 split; test rows are never touched during tuning.

02 — Scorer

Eight weighted, readable signals

Device, address, amount, customer age, order history, velocity, shared devices, and purchase hour. Threshold picked on train only, then locked before touching test.

03 — Decision

Flag or clear — never act

Above threshold, a transaction is flagged and surfaced to a human with its reasons. The system never blocks, refunds, or moves money on its own — defense-only, by design.
